# Code of Conduct

900Notes is a public project for people with different backgrounds, languages, resources, and levels of experience. Participation must remain useful and safe.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect and discuss the work, not the person.
- Make room for questions and explain unfamiliar terms when practical.
- Accept correction, give credit, and keep review comments specific.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's private information.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer says it is disruptive or unwelcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

For conduct in a public issue or pull request, use GitHub's report feature and contact the maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Do not start a public argument about a report. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports are reviewed as privately as the platform permits. A maintainer named in a report must not make the enforcement decision alone.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
