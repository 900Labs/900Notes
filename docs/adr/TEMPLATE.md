# Architecture Decision Record Template

## ADR-NNNN: Title

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | rejected | deprecated | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: Names of people involved in the decision
- **Context**: Sprint or phase where this decision is being made

---

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that are driving this decision. What is the issue we're facing? What alternatives exist? What constraints do we have (offline-first, low-resource hardware, no cloud, Apache 2.0)?

## Decision

State the decision clearly. What are we doing? Be specific about the chosen approach.

## Consequences

### Positive
- What benefits does this decision bring?

### Negative
- What trade-offs are we accepting?

### Neutral
- What side effects should we be aware of?

## Alternatives Considered

List the alternatives that were evaluated and why they were not chosen. Be specific about the rejection criteria.

## References

- Links to relevant documentation, discussions, or external resources
- Related ADRs (if any)
